## Texto

Hi everyone, and thank you for stopping by!

**Project Ciel 0.1.0 is now available.** It is an AI companion that lives on your own PC: you create a
character, chat with it, talk to it out loud, let it remember you, and bring it to your Twitch or Discord
community. No account, no subscription, and your data stays on your computer.

### What is in this first version

- **Characters** with their own identity, 19 personality sliders, mood, avatar and theme.
- **Chat and voice calls** with speech recognition and text-to-speech.
- **Memory** you can review and mark as private or public.
- **Twitch and Discord** support, with emotes and a viewer registry. Only public memories are used there.
- **OBS avatar** that reacts while your character thinks and talks.
- **Bring your own AI**: local models (LM Studio, Ollama and similar) or your own OpenAI, Anthropic and
  Google Gemini keys, with automatic fallback if one runs out of credits.
- **Full backups**, character export and import, and a diagnostics center.
- **6 languages**: Spanish, English, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

### Before you start

- Project Ciel **does not include an AI model**. You connect your own, local or cloud.
- Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* because the installer is not code-signed yet. Click
  **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Installing through the itch.io app avoids it.
- This is version 0.1, so a few rough edges remain: loud keyboard noises can be picked up as speech in
  calls (headphones help), the echo guard is not true echo cancellation, and Discord is text-only.

### What comes next

I would like to hear what you want most before deciding the order. For now I am watching feedback on
setup, voice calls and the Twitch integration. Bugs and ideas are very welcome in the Community tab.

If you try it, tell me what character you made. I would love to hear about it.

Thank you for supporting an independent project.
